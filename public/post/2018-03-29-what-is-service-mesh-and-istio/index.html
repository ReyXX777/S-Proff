Preparation Fun Friends

A journey through wisdom, insights, and preparation tales.

The roadmap that shaped the path.

My Preparation Materials

Preparing for NEET PG isn’t just about hard work — it’s about being smart with your resources. I try to carefully manage which materials I use for each subject, depending on what fits best. Personally, I prefer Marrow videos and notes as my core resources, but I also make sure to explore other valuable content from BTR and various free online tutorials and courses.

To crack NEET PG, you have to be focused, passionate, and wise in your preparation choices. It’s not just about what you study, but how you approach it.

Also, discussing topics with my roommate, who’s an amazing friend and a huge support, makes a world of difference. We help each other stay sharp, motivated, and positive — and we truly believe we’re going to make it. You can too. Believe in yourself. Trust your preparation.

My Favorite Cat

When I first met her, she was the best cat from day one. I watched her grow up alongside her siblings, and it was pure joy to be a part of her journey. She eventually had her own babies, and we lovingly gave names to both her and her little ones.

Most evenings at sunset, we would play with her — carrying her, chasing her around, laughing as we watched her gently (and sometimes fiercely!) intimidate her kittens. She held a very special place in our hearts, and I know she always will.

Sadly, she passed away a few months ago, and it really broke my heart. I miss her so much. But the memories we shared continue to warm my soul every day.

Hostel Life

If it wasn’t for my friends, I wouldn’t have had such a great hostel life. While the facilities and amenities around us aren’t exactly the best, we manage it all with a sense of purpose and a lot of laughter.

Even though I’m not very religious, whenever there’s a festival happening, I make it a point to join in. Maybe it’s the spirit of the occasion — it gives me a sense of patience and quiet strength that helps me keep going.

Hostel life isn’t perfect, but with the right people around, it becomes a memory you’ll always cherish.

Keeping Sanity

Medical life can be incredibly tough, but you should never let the pressure defeat you. Always remember — you’re here for a purpose, and that purpose is one of the noblest there is: saving and healing lives.

There’s a certain joy in that certainty, a quiet pride that makes the journey worth it.

Keep following me as we explore little nuggets of wisdom, the art of learning, and of course, finding moments of fun along the way.
